Linear dose–response meta-analysis

Two-stage generalized least squares (GLS) with fixed-effect or DerSimonian–Laird random-effects pooling.

Input must include the within-study covariance matrices of log-relative-risk contrasts. Category-specific 95% CIs alone do not establish those correlations. This tool does not reconstruct Greenland–Longnecker covariances, fit splines, or make clinical decisions.
